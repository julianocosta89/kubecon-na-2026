SCENARIO 04: RUNG 4+, ADAPTIVE TAIL SAMPLING

THE IDEA
  Scenario 03 keeps every error and 10% of the rest. "10%" is a promise about
  a ratio, not about your bill: if traffic triples, so does what you keep.
  Adaptive tail sampling keeps a promise about volume instead:

    keep-errors   any span with status ERROR   → always kept, as before
    budget        everything else shares ~15 spans/s per gateway pod

  It learns a rate for each kind of trace (fingerprint: the service that
  started it + the routes it touched) and re-tunes every 15 s. A kind that
  suddenly floods in gets sampled hard; rare kinds keep a bigger share, so
  the unusual stuff stays visible when the boring stuff spikes.

  Same gateway tier as 03, same agents. Only the gateway's processor changes:
  tail_sampling → adaptive_tail_sampling (otelcol-contrib v0.163+, alpha).

  Optional, same as 03: paymentFailure → 100%; every failure is still kept.
  The flood is heavy on a laptop. Turn it off once the point is made.

THE TRADE-OFF
  You pay a fixed amount, not a fixed fraction, so the share of a flood you
  keep goes down as the flood goes up. Every kind of trace keeps a minimum,
  so it settles near the budget, not exactly on it. And it's alpha: it
  shipped in otelcol-contrib v0.163.0 (October 2026).
