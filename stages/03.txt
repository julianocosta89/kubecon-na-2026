SCENARIO 03: RUNG 4, TAIL SAMPLING

THE IDEA
  Wait until the whole trace is in, then decide with full knowledge:
    keep-errors      any span with status ERROR   → always kept
    keep-slow        trace longer than 2 s        → always kept
    sample-the-rest  10% of everything else

  To see a whole trace, one Collector must get all of its spans. A trace's spans
  come from services on different workers, so they land on different agents.
  The agents therefore route every span by trace ID to one of the 2 gateway
  pods ("{{GW}}"), which holds each trace for 10 s and decides.

  The noise filter from scenario 02 stays on the agents; the probabilistic
  sampler is replaced by the gateway's policies. Span metrics stay complete.
