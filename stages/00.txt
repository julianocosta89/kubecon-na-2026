THE IDEA
  Every library is instrumented, nothing is filtered, nothing is sampled. This
  is the bill the next scenarios cut. Each one moves the "keep or drop"
  decision to a different point of the pipeline:

    app ──► network ──► Collector (agents) ──► gateway ──► Jaeger
     01                  02                     03  tail sampling (10% of the rest)
     don't create it     filter + sample 10%    04  adaptive (a budget, not a %)

  The earlier you decide, the more you save, and the less you know when you decide.

WHAT IS RUNNING
  • kind cluster "kubecon-sampling": 1 control plane + 3 workers
  • OpenTelemetry Demo 3.1.0: ~20 services, the load generator shops nonstop (10 users)
  • Collector agents: one per worker (DaemonSet) → Jaeger, plus span metrics → Prometheus
  • Gateway Collector: 2 pods, installed but idle until scenario 03
  • Perses: the live dashboard, reading Prometheus and Jaeger
