SCENARIO 02: RUNGS 2+3, FILTER THEN SAMPLE 10%

THE IDEA
  Scenario 01's switches stay on; everything else the apps create reaches the
  Collector, and the Collector decides. Two steps, both cheap:

  Rung 2, filter: drop what you can name. Here: the proxy tracing our own
    tooling (browser telemetry uploads, the Jaeger UI, the flag UI, the
    load-generator UI). One OTTL rule. Telemetry about telemetry; nobody debugs it.
  Rung 3, probabilistic sampling: keep 10% of traces. The sampler hashes the
    trace ID, so all three agents agree on every trace without talking to each
    other, and nothing has to be remembered.

  Span metrics are computed before both steps, so request and error rates stay
  complete. That's why the traces pipeline is split in two in the diff below.

THE BLIND SPOT
  1. Break payments: {{SHOP}}/feature → paymentFailure → 100%
     The shop only places ~6 orders a minute (10 users), so 100% means
     ~6 failures a minute. 50% leaves too few to see.
  2. Perses: set the time range to "Last 5 minutes", then row 3, "The failure":
       left   "Failed payments": every failure, counted by span metrics
       right  the failed payments you can debug: their traces in Jaeger,
              about 1 in 10 of them
     Read it as two numbers: "30 payments failed. You get 3 to debug with."
     Turn the flag on as soon as the scenario is live, so a few minutes of
     failures pile up before you point at it (~6 failures a minute).
  3. Switch paymentFailure back to off.
